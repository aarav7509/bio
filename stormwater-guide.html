What is recorded % of shown manholes

Seven fields are checked: owner, reliability, diameter, depth, surface level, invert level and lid type. A value counts as recorded only if it is present and plausible. Placeholder values such as a depth of 999.99 count as missing.

"Unknown" means the information is not recorded in this register. It does not mean the asset has no owner or that nobody is responsible for it. Locations are as recorded and may be approximate. To report a problem with a manhole, or to check an asset near your property, contact Brisbane City Council. This is an independent project and is not affiliated with Brisbane City Council.
